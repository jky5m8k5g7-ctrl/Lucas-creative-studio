# First real run from an idea (2026-09-25)

Idea (fictional brand): "Solace makes a $38 ceramic pour-over dripper, glazed by hand, one cup at
a time. The ten minutes of making coffee by hand are the only part of a parent's morning nobody
interrupts." Command `IDEA`, review `gates` (stops at the route choice), budget 30 calls.
Full output: `projects/test-solace/` (`package.md`, `state.json`).

| Strategy | 7 → 7 → 7 | below A |
| Routes | 7 → 7 → 7 | below A |

18 agent calls, 2 h 33 min, about 1.9M tokens. No code check failed.

The development stage found a strong, specific idea ("The Tape Line": the family queues
behind a strip of painter's tape until the last drop, then crosses before her first sip).

## What went wrong

1. **Agents explored the repository.** 91 tool calls across 18 agents: reading the workflow
   code, the long reference briefs and old projects, running shell maths, and one created a
   suggested-task card. Calls took 5–18 minutes each, and reviewers graded against the 40 KB
   reference briefs the studio deliberately keeps out of prompts.
2. **Stages did later departments' work.** Development and the routes carried lens choices,
   blur in pixels and frame-accurate timings; reviewers then asked for more of it (a lens for
   camera A, staging for 26 shots), so strategy and routes could not reach 8 on craft.

## Fixes (next commit)

- Every prompt now opens with an instruction to work only from the prompt and use no tools
  (the isolation that kept the taste test clean).
- Each department has a lane: what it decides, what it leaves to later departments, and who
  must be able to act on it. Makers are told to stay in it; reviewers judge each stage at its
  own level, never ask for later departments' decisions, and treat such detail as scope creep.
- Reviewers give at most six notes, most score-moving first.
- `spec/quality-bar.md`: specificity is judged at each department's own stage.
