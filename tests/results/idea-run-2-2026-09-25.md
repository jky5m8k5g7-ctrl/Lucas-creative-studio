# Second real run from an idea (2026-09-25)

Same idea and settings as run 1, with agents kept off the repository (no tools) and each
department given a lane. Full output: `projects/test-solace-2/`.

## Result

| Stage | Review rounds (lowest score) | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Development | 6 → 6 → 6 | below A |
| Strategy | 7 → 7 → 7 | below A |
| Routes | 6 → 7 → 7 | below A |

18 agent calls, 1 h 25 min (run 1: 2 h 33 min), about 1.2M tokens (run 1: 1.9M). Tool calls: 18,
one structured answer per agent (run 1: 91). Development again found the painter's-tape idea on
its own ("Last Drip").

## What went wrong

1. **Reviewers couldn't see the assumptions.** Each department writes its assumptions (A01…)
   and blockers in the envelope, but the reviewer, the reviser and later departments were only
   given the content. Development's craft score of 6 was "there is no A07 on the page"; the
   strategist was marked down for "missing assumptions and blockers sections". A pipeline bug,
   not the agents' work.
2. **Each review round started fresh.** Reviewers raised new issues instead of checking whether
   their earlier notes were fixed, so scores didn't move across rounds.
3. **The strategist had no lane.** The lane meant for it was inserted into the route summary
   given to later departments instead.

The remaining notes are real creative ones: the proposition drifting from Lucas's line, a
recommended route that fails the rival-swap test, two routes whose cutdowns were the same film.

## Fixes (next commit)

- Reviewers, revisers, the integrity check, the panel and downstream departments get each
  department's work with its assumptions and blockers.
- From the second round on, the reviewer gets its previous scores and notes, checks each one
  first, and raises new notes only for problems as serious.
- The strategist's lane is on the strategist; a test checks every department has one.
