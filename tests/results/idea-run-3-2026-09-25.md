## What it made

- Development chose a **social series**, "Hold That Thought": each episode one child waits at
  a tape line with news that can't wait (a lost tooth, an unsigned slip, home-cut bangs) until
  the last drop leaves the dripper. It tied the series rhythm to the brand's own words, "one
  cup at a time".
- Strategy: "Their news keeps, one cup at a time."
- Routes: R1 "News that can't wait can, for one cup, and it's still news when the dripper
  lifts." R2 "The quiet isn't taken from the kids; they're the ones who keep it, one cup at a
  time." R3 (recommended) "Answering mid-pour gets them a glance; finishing the cup gets them
  your eyes."
